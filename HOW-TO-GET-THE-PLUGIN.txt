GET THE PLUGIN WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING
===========================================

1. Go to github.com and make a free account (or log in).
2. Click the "+" at the top right -> "New repository".
   Name it VioletTune, leave everything else, click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file".
4. Unzip VioletTune.zip, then drag EVERYTHING inside the VioletTune folder
   (Source, .github, CMakeLists.txt) into the browser page.
   Make sure the ".github" folder is included.
5. Click "Commit changes".
6. Click the "Actions" tab at the top. A build starts by itself
   (yellow dot = building, green check = done, takes ~5 minutes).
7. Click the finished build, scroll to "Artifacts" at the bottom,
   click "VioletTune-VST3" to download a zip.
8. Unzip it. Copy the "VioletTune.vst3" FOLDER into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
9. Open FL Studio -> Options -> Manage plugins -> "Find plugins".
   Add VioletTune to a vocal channel's effect slot.

If the build shows a red X, click it, copy the error text,
and paste it to me. I'll fix the code.
